Questions we hear every week

If yours is not here, ask it. We answer every message ourselves, usually the same day.

Ask your own
Do you only sell architect-designed homes?

Mostly, but not only. We take on houses with a clear idea behind them: how they sit on the land and how the light moves through them. Some were drawn by well-known names, some by the owners themselves.

What does a viewing at the right hour mean?

Every house has a time of day it was built for. We find out when that is and book your visit then, so you see the house the way you would live in it.

Can we rent before we buy?

Yes. About a third of our buyers start with a six to twelve month let in the same area. Part of the rent can count toward the fee if you later buy with us.

How long does a search usually take?

Between six weeks and four months from the first call to an accepted offer. Searches from far away take a little longer, because we film more.

Do you help once the keys are handed over?

We stay in touch through the first season and introduce the builders, gardeners and caretakers we have worked with for years.

Selling a house with a story?

We write about each home the way its architect would, photograph it at its best hour and show it only to people who already understand it.
